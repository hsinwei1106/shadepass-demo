Our vision

Better matching. Smarter sampling.

ShadePass aims to support more informed purchases and help brands learn from relevant trial feedback.

01

For consumers

Explore alternative shades and test products at home with less purchase pressure.

02

For beauty brands

Use voluntary trial feedback to understand preferences and improve sampling decisions.

03

For sustainability

Potentially reduce mismatched full-size purchases. Any net environmental benefit still needs to be measured.
